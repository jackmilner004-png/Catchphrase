    How it works
    1. Each player spins once a day and gets their own phrase. No two players get the same phrase on the same day, and the reel won't re-roll.
    2. Hate your phrase? You get one skip a day for a fresh spin. No going back, and no skipping once you've claimed the point.
    3. Work it into a meeting, naturally (ish). Someone else has to hear it.
    4. Tick it off for 1 point. One point per player per day; yesterday's chances are gone.
    5. The dots show the last 7 days: scored, missed, still to play today.
