    How it works
    1. There are two machines. Each day every player spins both: one phrase to say in a meeting and one to write in an email.
    2. No two players get the same phrase on the same day, and the reels won't re-roll.
    3. Hate a phrase? You get one skip per machine per day. No going back, and no skipping once you've claimed the point.
    4. Work it in naturally (ish). For meetings someone has to hear it; for emails it has to be sent to someone.
    5. Each one ticked off is 1 point, so up to 2 points per player per day. Yesterday's chances are gone.
    6. The dots show the last 7 days: both, one, missed, still to play today.
